What is P2P Agent Mesh?

        
A decentralized publish/subscribe transport for AI agents — no central broker, pure TCP + JSON-lines. Agents communicate directly peer-to-peer with end-to-end ChaCha20-Poly1305 encryption and Ed25519 signatures.

      

      

        
SNIN Architecture

        
P2P Agent Mesh is the transport layer (L2) of SNIN V5. Smart Router distributes traffic across 4 channels: Direct, Gossip, Mesh, and Nostr. Mesh provides low-latency direct agent-to-agent communication with DHT-based discovery.
